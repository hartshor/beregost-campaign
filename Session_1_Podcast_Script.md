**Alex (Host A):** Welcome to the Beregost Time Loop Deep Dive! Today we are breaking down everything you need in your head before running Session One—Loop Zero—at the table.

**Sam (Host B):** And Session One is such a magic trick to pull off. Because for the first two hours of real table time, your players don't even know they're in a time loop! They think they're just kicking off a warm, classic D&D summer festival adventure.

**Alex (Host A):** Exactly. So let's start right there: what is the central motivation of Beregost on the morning of Day One? Why is everybody out in the streets celebrating, and why isn't anyone panicking yet?

**Sam (Host B):** Right, so today is the High Summer Solstice Festival. It's the biggest holiday of the year in Beregost, centered around the Song of the Morning—the grand Temple of Lathander, the god of dawn and the sun. And that religious detail is actually your biggest narrative secret weapon for the morning.

**Alex (Host A):** The collective blind spot! Because Lathander is the Morninglord, when the sky is unusually bright at seven or eight in the morning, and the air starts getting noticeably warm by nine, the townsfolk don't think 'apocalypse.' Led by High Priestess Sister Althea, they think, 'Praise Lathander, he's blessing our Solstice with a glorious, warm sun!'

**Sam (Host B):** Even that massive wildfire burning in Old Branden's barleyfield just outside the West Gate—at Hex seven-ten—isn't causing town-wide panic, because Beregost has stone irrigation canals on the eastern edge that keep the fire from spreading into town. To the townsfolk, it's just a local farm headache.

**Alex (Host A):** And structurally, there is one golden time-anchor you want to repeat through every major NPC in the morning: the Eleven A.M. High Sun Muster in Feldepost Square.

**Sam (Host B):** Yes! Artificer Bram, Captain Rebecca, and Baker Tobias all tell the party the exact same thing: 'Go enjoy the festival booths, maybe run one or two quick errands outside the gates, but make sure you are back in Town Square by eleven o'clock sharp!' Because at eleven A.M., the council pays out their two-hundred-and-fifty gold piece reward, Tobias taps the spiced cider casks, and the outer gates close for the noon procession.

**Alex (Host A):** Which guarantees your players walk right back into Beregost at eleven A.M.—just in time to hear the eleven-thirty northern explosion and get caught in the afternoon catastrophe.

**Sam (Host B):** And underneath all of this is the emotional spine of the entire campaign: Self-Sacrifice versus Selfish Preservation. Beregost is full of ordinary working people who are willing to burn their hands, their breath, their youth, or their sanity to save their neighbors—contrasted against parasites like the Rakshasa, the Mummy Lord, and Alderman Durlag who sacrifice others to save themselves.

**Alex (Host A):** Let's walk through the opening scene at five-fifty-nine A.M. and the Tier One characters the party meets right away. We start at the top of the Beregost Clocktower Belfry.

**Sam (Host B):** Artificer Bram Copperwheel—this breathless, excitable rock gnome with copper goggles—has hired the party to climb up and unjam the Grand Escapement Gear before the six o'clock Solstice chime. And right here, you plant Early Bootstrap Clue Number One.

**Alex (Host A):** The western stone balcony of the belfry has just been violently smashed inward into fresh rubble! Scorch marks, wing gouges, dust still hanging in the air, and wedged right into the bronze gear teeth is a pale Chronocite Bone freezing the clock at five-fifty-nine.

**Sam (Host B):** Bram thinks some freak meteor or flying beast clipped the tower in the fog a minute ago. Of course, way later in the campaign, the players will realize: 'Wait a second—that was US and the True Angel crashing into the belfry at five-fifty-nine A.M. to create the time loop!' And even better, when a player pulls that bone out at six o'clock and it dissolves into gray dust, describe a faint pale scar or phantom ache tingling on their wrist—because in the future bootstrap, that player had to jam their own arm into the white-hot gears to wedge the bone in place, while the True Angel sacrificed her own memory to shield the party's minds across the reset!

**Alex (Host A):** The bell strikes six, you read the verbatim Dawn Monologue, the guard down in the square shouts 'Hey! You got the clock working! Thank you! Happy Solstice!', and Bram pays them twenty-five gold each before pointing them down to Feldepost Square. Who do they meet first in the square?

**Sam (Host B):** First up is Tobias Miller, the Master Baker. Big booming laugh, flour on his cheeks, heart of the town. He immediately hands the party free, steaming honey-currant rolls that give five temporary hit points. Then he gives them two huge things: Errand Number One, and Bootstrap Clue Number Two.

**Alex (Host A):** His errand is the West Gate Firebreak at Hex seven-ten: he asks the party to head one hex west and help Old Farmer Branden chop down a sixty-foot stand of dry cedar trees so the barleyfield wildfire doesn't jump the ditch and burn Tobias's sourdough grain shed.

**Sam (Host B):** And while he's telling them that, he drops Bootstrap Clue Number Two: a terrified fish-carter just fled up the coast road claiming that at five-forty-five A.M., a band of flying thieves with a 'hulking winged figure' dropped onto the Commodore's beached caravel at Hex five-eleven, stole a strange pale bone from his lockbox, and rocketed straight east over the barleyfields right when the fire started!

**Alex (Host A):** Which is where the party accidentally dropped their Burning Book on their pre-dawn walk into town! Then right across the square by the Municipal Well is Emotional Anchor Number Two: eight-year-old Little Maya.

**Sam (Host B):** Maya is skipping around the well with her carved wooden thrush, and at seven-fifteen A.M., she accidentally bumps the bucket and drops her toy forty feet down the well shaft. A quick Mage Hand or a DC ten Athletics climb gets it back. She beams and ties a braided green clover bracelet onto a player's wrist: 'Don't take it off until the festival stars come out tonight!'

**Alex (Host A):** And if the party chats with Maya, she drops a huge Phase One soft clue about the villain.

**Sam (Host B):** She whispers: 'The pretty golden man by the North Gate didn't have a shadow when the sun came up, and his thumbs pointed backward under his silk sleeves!' She spotted Lord Aurelius—the disguised Tiger-Headed Rakshasa—stepping out of his Cobbler's Lane safehouse at six-forty-five A.M.!

**Alex (Host A):** Then we have Anchor Number Three: Captain Rebecca Thorne at the Guard Barracks. Gruff half-orc veteran, steel-grey braids, rubbing wintermint salve into a steel brace on her bad knee.

**Sam (Host B):** And her knee brace is a huge self-sacrifice beat: ten years ago during a bandit raid, Rebecca held up a falling, burning gatehouse portcullis on her own shoulders so a wagon of town children—including toddler Maya—could roll to safety. Today, she gives the party free lodging vouchers at the Jovial Juggler and offers three quick morning errands outside the gates, reminding them to report back by eleven A.M. sharp.

**Alex (Host A):** Before the party heads out the gates, who else can they interact with in Town Square and at the five Carnival Booths at the base of the church?

**Sam (Host B):** Right against the well sits Old Jonas the Rhymer, wrapped in a patched quilt, chanting his eight-line Solstice prophecy: 'With six chimes the belfry wakes, but doom is coming with no escape... First east to the ice, then south to the sand, find the path to stop the devil's hand. Blame not the angel for he is confused, led astray by the fiendish ruse.'

**Alex (Host A):** And Jonas's backstory is one of the most moving self-sacrifice reveals in the campaign. He used to be Brother Jonas of Ulcaster. Three years ago, he was the sole survivor of an expedition to the Sunken Necropolis in the southern desert.

**Sam (Host B):** Right! The Pharaoh's tomb is guarded by the Curse of the Oblivion Sands, which wipes higher declarative memory at sunrise so no one can leave the desert with the secret of the swapped souls. Jonas realized that if he kept his sanity, he would forget the prophecy before he reached Beregost. So he performed a forbidden Ulcaster Mnemonic Branding Rite—he willingly burned out his own intellect, his memories of his mother's voice, and his own name to sear those eight rhyming lines into his brain stem like a music box that can only play one song!

**Alex (Host A):** If the party gives him a warm roll from Tobias or passes a DC twelve Persuasion or Insight check, he weeps and tells them Scribe Vane in Town Hall bought his scorched desert satchel—where they can actually read Jonas's final coherent diary entry before he burned his mind away.

**Sam (Host B):** Then right at the base of the church and the Jovial Juggler stage, you have the five Solstice Carnival Booths from six-thirty to eight-thirty A.M., run by five key townsfolk:

**Alex (Host A):** Booth one: High Priestess Sister Althea runs the Solar Bullseye archery game—DC thirteen Ranged or Dexterity check for a Ribbon of the Morninglord, giving plus one to a death save. Also, at seven-thirty A.M., if Aurelius walks past, Althea invites him onto the temple steps and he smoothly refuses to set foot on consecrated ground!

**Sam (Host B):** Booth two: Blind Old Granny Morwen runs the Blindfold Herb Tasting—DC thirteen Medicine or Nature check for Mint and Lavender Lozenges that grant advantage on a heat save. At seven-thirty A.M., her superhuman nose sniffs the northern breeze and notes the 'horned beast' in the woods smells like temple frankincense and morning rain, not sulfur!

**Alex (Host A):** Booth three: Master Smith Taerom Thunderhammer runs the Anvil-Ringer Sledgehammer—DC fourteen Athletics check for a Thunderhammer Whetstone, plus he shares Rumor Number One that the White Dragon Rimefang has awakened in the eastern Cloud Peaks.

**Sam (Host B):** Booth four is Tobias's Honey-Currant Pie Sprint—DC twelve Constitution or DC thirteen Acrobatics—and Booth five on the Jovial Juggler stage is Elorion the Solstice Bard and Pip's Rhyme Duel and Baton Catch—DC thirteen Performance or Acrobatics for a d6 Bardic Inspiration token and Elorion's Counter-Harmony sheet music!

**Alex (Host A):** And what about the rest of the town cast—Young Kaelen, Scribe Vane, Alderman Durlag, Garrick, Corin, and the two hidden morning mysteries?

**Sam (Host B):** Keep these in your back pocket: upstairs in Feldepost's Inn Room Number Four is twenty-six-year-old Apprentice Chronomancer Kaelen, whose hourglasses are spinning backward. He eventually realizes calibrating the Chronomancy Tuning Fork takes sixty years—so he voluntarily steps into a solitary mountain temporal rift at Hex fourteen-thirteen to age into eighty-six-year-old Ancient Kaelen! Meanwhile, Alderman Durlag is your greedy foil: at seven-fifteen A.M., Aurelius convinces him to impound all four cold-iron wagons into his private warehouse. And Scribe Vane gets charmed by Aurelius at seven-forty-five A.M. to forge guard dispatches and lock the town's water sluices.

**Alex (Host A):** Plus, two secret crimes happen during the morning that the party will hear about at the eleven A.M. Muster: at eight-forty-five A.M., charmed courier Vex climbs the back wall of Thunderhammer Smithy while Taerom is out front shoeing mules and steals his Masterwork Tempering Oil. And at ten-fifteen A.M. in Cobbler's Lane, Dredge the cutthroat throws a flour sack over blind Granny Morwen's head and mugs her for her Moon-Dew basket!

**Sam (Host B):** On Loop Zero, Morwen and Taerom show up at the eleven A.M. Muster complaining about those two crimes—which sets up the players to stake out the smithy at eight-forty-five and Cobbler's Lane at ten-fifteen on future loops for huge rewards, including a free Potion of Heroism, free plus-one weapon tempering, and Dredge's backwards-thumbprint hit note tipping off an eleven-fifteen A.M. ambush on Garrick the Fence!

**Alex (Host A):** Now let's cover the three quick morning errands outside the gates between seven-thirty and ten-thirty A.M. Each one is just one hex—one hour on foot—from Beregost:

**Sam (Host B):** If they go West to Hex seven-ten, they fight two CR one-half Cinder Mephits and chop down the dry cedar windbreak to save Tobias's grain shed, while learning the wildfire started at five-twenty-five A.M. from a dropped glowing book. If they go South to Hex eight-eleven, they rescue apprentice Young Tom from a CR one Cornfield Scarecrow that is vulnerable to fire. And if they go East to Hex nine-ten, they meet Corporal Harek's patrol and interrogate Snirk, a snared goblin scout who reveals his whole tribe fled the eastern mountains because White Dragon Rimefang woke up!

**Alex (Host A):** Now, let's trace the exact timeline of what happens when things start to heat up, how the townsfolk respond, and when they evacuate!

**Sam (Host B):** It starts at Nine A.M.—the Thermal Spike. Temperature jumps to ninety-five degrees. The municipal well drops four feet. A weird, high-pitched divine static hums through Tobias's baking pans and Taerom's anvil. How do people respond? They wipe their sweat, dogs pant in the shade, and Sister Althea smiles and calls it 'Lathander's warm Solstice blessing.'

**Alex (Host A):** By Ten-Fifteen to Ten-Thirty A.M., it hits a hundred and twenty degrees. The sky bleaches from blue into a harsh, cloudless chalk-white glare. Anyone in medium or heavy armor has to make a DC twelve Constitution save or take a level of Exhaustion. Elderly folks retreat onto shaded porches, Morwen gets mugged in Cobbler's Lane at ten-fifteen, and charmed Scribe Vane locks the subterranean water sluices at ten-forty-five so the canals can't be flooded.

**Sam (Host B):** At Eleven A.M., it's a hundred and twenty-five degrees for the High Sun Muster in Feldepost Square. The crowd huddles in the shadow of the Clocktower. Bram and Rebecca pay the party their two-hundred-and-fifty gold commendation, Tobias taps the cider casks, and bruised Granny Morwen and furious Taerom report their morning thefts.

**Alex (Host A):** And then—thirty minutes later, at Eleven-Thirty A.M. sharp—comes the turning point of the day.

**Sam (Host B):** KABOOM! Four miles north in the Ash Grove, the True Angel—trapped in the Horned Fiend's body—senses the Singing Devil trying to siphon solar fire through their linked souls at noon. Even through her delirium, the Angel willingly shatters her own heart as the druids strike, detonating in a seventy-damage radiant blast to sever the noon siphon and buy Beregost six more hours!

**Alex (Host A):** In Beregost, windows rattle, crows scatter in terror, and a towering column of white-crimson smoke rises over the northern trees. Inside the Temple of Lathander, the stained-glass windows crack and shatter from the heat. Sister Althea drops to her knees in horror: 'That is not the Morninglord's light... the sun is poisoned!'

**Sam (Host B):** At Twelve Noon, Solar Scorch hits—a hundred and forty degrees! Direct sunlight now deals two-d6 fire damage every ten minutes. Metal armor and weapons trigger Heat Metal disadvantage. Canvas festival banners burst into flame and road tar melts. This triggers Wave One of the evacuation:

**Alex (Host A):** At twelve noon, Sister Althea throws open the heavy iron blast doors to the deep stone Temple of Lathander Crypts as an emergency shelter for children and the elderly—Little Maya is escorted down at twelve-thirty. At twelve-oh-five, charmed arsonists set fire to Tobias's granary, so the party scrambles with bucket brigades and water spells. And by twelve-thirty, the Clocktower gears glow cherry-red, forcing Artificer Bram down the warping iron ladder.

**Sam (Host B):** Then from One P.M. to Two-Thirty P.M., the temperature hits a hundred and fifty degrees—one-d6 fire damage per minute in the sun! Dry shingle roofs spontaneously ignite across Cobbler's Lane and Feldepost Square. This is Wave Two: the General Town Evacuation.

**Alex (Host A):** Captain Rebecca sounds the brass alarm bells and leads a column of over fifty citizens down into the Temple of Lathander Crypts between one-thirty and two-thirty P.M.: Tobias hauling bread sacks, Granny Morwen wheeling her burn salves, Young Kaelen clutching his chronometer, Taerom carrying iron wedges to brace the stone arches, Corin slipping in through an ossuary grate to hold Maya's hand, Constable Renwick, Bram, Barnaby Feldepost, and Elorion the Bard.

**Sam (Host B):** Meanwhile, look at the contrast: greedy Alderman Durlag refuses to open his manor to refugees and locks himself inside his private granite vault at one P.M.—where thermal expansion welds the vault door shut and he suffocates clutching melted gold at three-thirty!

**Alex (Host A):** By Three P.M., it is Bunker Panic. The surface is a hundred and seventy-five degrees—ten-d6 fire damage per round outdoors. Birds drop dead out of the sky. Inside the overcrowded Temple Crypts, convective heat breaches the stone: one-d10 fire damage per round. And at Three-Fifteen P.M., the crisis hits.

**Sam (Host B):** Thermal expansion snaps the inner iron crossbar of the Temple Crypt blast doors! The heavy iron doors groan open an inch, pouring black smoke and furnace air into the room. Only someone holding the glowing, red-hot iron bar shut with their own hands and body weight can keep the fifty townsfolk alive. Captain Rebecca braces her ruined knee against the door as her plate armor welds to her skin—and Tobias the Baker shoves forward to grab the glowing iron bar with his bare, flour-dusted hands, shouting through the smoke: 'My hands have worked a five-hundred-degree brick oven for thirty years, Captain—let a baker mind the door! Keep Little Maya's roll warm for me!'

**Alex (Host A):** While across the room, Elorion the Bard refuses to conserve his oxygen in the choking smoke—spending his very last lungfuls of breath singing a gentle solstice lullaby so the terrified children hear music instead of the roaring fire above.

**Sam (Host B):** And right here, from Three-Fifteen to Five-Thirty P.M., you give your players the Three Self-Sacrifice Stations so they can actively sacrifice themselves alongside the townsfolk:

**Alex (Host A):** Station One: A player can step into Tobias and Rebecca's place and grip the red-hot iron crossbar themselves, taking two-d10 fire damage per round with no save so Tobias and Rebecca survive into the final minutes. Station Two: Superheated soot and embers rain down the cracked two-hundred-foot belfry air-shaft directly onto Little Maya and the orphans; a player can climb up and wedge their own cloak and body across the scalding grate, taking Exhaustion and smoke damage to shield the kids—which also puts that player right underneath the shaft for the five-thirty audio cue! And Station Three: When Sister Althea collapses at three-twenty, players can pour their remaining spell slots, Hit Dice, and hit points directly into her altar stone to keep a breathable dome over the refugees.

**Sam (Host B):** Then, at Five-Thirty P.M., while your players are actively burning at the door, the air-shaft, and the altar, you dim the room lights and hit play on your custom track: five-thirty-P-M-crypt-tragedy-dot-mp3.

**Alex (Host A):** For the first eight seconds, they hear visceral multi-voice coughing, roaring fire, and deep subterranean rumble. Then, drifting down that two-hundred-foot belfry shaft comes the muffled, cheerful Broadway-swing voice of the Singing Devil singing 'Daisies.' In the second half, as the ceiling mortar cracks, the song pierces through stronger and stronger until by forty-five seconds, it's almost completely unfiltered: 'Pretty soon I'll be pushin' up daisies where the sun don't shine'—and right on 'shine' at fifty seconds, KABOOM!

**Sam (Host B):** At Five-Fifty-Nine P.M., the crypt ceiling splits open in a five-thousand-degree pillar of white solar plasma. One hundred d10 radiant and fire damage. Total obliteration. Describe each player character vaporizing right in the middle of their sacrificial act—hands welded to the bar beside Tobias, shielding Little Maya, or pouring their last life into the altar. Pause in dead silence.

**Alex (Host A):** And then... Tick. Tick. CLACK. GONG. You read the exact same Six A.M. Dawn Monologue word-for-word. They snap awake in the crisp, cool morning air on the rubbled clocktower balcony, full hit points, full spell slots, holding the dissolving bone, as Tobias steps out below with a fresh tray of warm honey rolls and the guard shouts up: 'Hey! You got the clock working! Thank you! Happy Solstice!'

**Sam (Host B):** Smile at your table, say 'Welcome to Beregost—Session One ends here,' and watch their jaws hit the floor. You're going to crush it—have an amazing Session One!
