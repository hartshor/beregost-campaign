4. Afternoon Heat, Cathedral Evacuation & Self-Sacrifice (10:00 AM – 5:59 PM)

Time & Heat What Happens & When to Evacuate to the Cathedral Townsfolk Response / Who Refuses to Evacuate
10:00 AM – 12:59 PM
Abnormally Warm (85–95°F)		 Sunny, abnormally warm Solstice day—no heat damage yet. No gryphon flight after 10:00 AM (violent upper-sky updrafts). 11:30 AM: Northern Ash Grove boom rattles windows. Townsfolk are happy! They laugh and praise Lathander for a glorious warm Solstice blessing (brushing off Old Jonas's warnings).
1:00 PM – 2:45 PM
Sweltering (120–135°F)		 Heat turns truly sweltering (harsh glare; heavy/medium armor DC 12 Con save vs Exhaustion; shingles smoke by 2:00 PM). EVACUATION WINDOW: At 1:00 PM, Sister Althea throws open the deep Cathedral (Temple of Lathander) Crypts; Captain Rebecca rings alarm bells and herds 50+ townsfolk underground from 1:30–2:45 PM! Seeing People Refuse to Evacuate (The Greedy Foil): While families rush to the Cathedral, the party sees Alderman Durlag Stonecrest slam his iron manor gates on begging neighbors, hoarding ice and gold inside his granite vault (door thermal-welds shut; suffocates at 3:30 PM). Charmed Scribe Vane hides in the Town Hall vault unless freed.
3:00 PM – 5:25 PM
Lethal Surface (175°F)		 MUST BE UNDERGROUND TO AVOID DAMAGE! Surface air deals 10d6 Fire/rd outdoors (1d10/rd in ground-level buildings); deep Cathedral sub-crypts stay insulated (0 dmg) until 5:30 PM—but at 3:15 PM, thermal expansion snaps the upper crypt blast door crossbar! Tobias & Rebecca's Stand: Rebecca braces her ruined war-knee against the scorching blast door while Tobias grips the red-hot iron bar in bare hands ("My hands know a 500° oven—let a baker mind the door!") so families can move further down. Elorion spends his last breath singing a lullaby in the smoke.
🔥 OPPORTUNITIES FOR PLAYER SELF-SACRIFICE WHILE EVACUATING FOLKS FURTHER DOWN (2:30 – 5:30 PM)
  • 1. Hold the Snapped, Red-Hot Cathedral Blast Door (3:15 PM): Step into Tobias & Rebecca's place holding the glowing iron crossbar (2d10 Fire/rd, no save) so Tobias, Rebecca, and the last stragglers can push further down into the deep sub-crypts alive.
  • 2. Dash Back for Stragglers & Shield the Cracked Belfry Air-Shaft: Run back into the scorching upper nave (2:45–3:00 PM) to carry fallen townsfolk down—or climb the stonework and wedge your cloak & body over the ember-raining belfry air-shaft above Little Maya & the orphans (voluntarily taking Exhaustion & 1d10 smoke/fire dmg/rd), placing that PC right beneath the shaft when "Daisies" plays at 5:30 PM!
  • 3. Soul-Burn Life Transference at Sister Althea's Altar: When Sister Althea collapses moving refugees deeper down, pour remaining Spell Slots, Lay on Hands, Hit Dice, & HP into her sun-disc to sustain a cool pocket until 5:30 PM (play 530pm_crypt_tragedy.mp3 → BOOM → 5:59 PM Whiteout → 6:00 AM Reset!).

📜 OLD JONAS THE RHYMER — THE 8-LINE SOLSTICE CHANT & TRIGGER HOOKS Must-Hear Clue

"With six chimes the belfry wakes,
But doom is coming with no escape.
Only one narrow path can provide salvation,
Threading this needle stops the devil's elation.

First east to the ice, then south to the sand,
Find the path to stop the devil's hand.
Blame not the angel for he is confused,
Led astray by the fiendish ruse."

3 Natural Hooks for When the Party Hears the Rhyme:
  • Hook A — 07:30 AM at Tobias's Bakery Porch (Recommended): While the party accepts warm honey-currant rolls from Tobias, haggard Old Jonas shuffles up shivering in his patched quilt. Tobias gently hands him a free roll; Jonas weeps into the bread and recites all 8 lines straight to the party while Tobias sighs ("Poor fellow—never quite right since he wandered back from the southern desert").
  • Hook B — 07:15 AM at the Municipal Well (After Maya's Thrush): Right as Little Maya ties the green clover bracelet onto a PC's wrist, Old Jonas (slumped against the stone well rim) catches the hem of a PC's cloak, stares wide-eyed at the clocktower above, and chants all 8 lines in a cracked singsong voice.
  • Hook C — 11:00–11:30 AM at the High Sun Muster (Failsafe): If the party bypassed him all morning, the instant the 11:30 AM northern explosion rattles Beregost's windows, Jonas scrambles onto the stone rim of the well pointing north and shouts the 8 lines over the startled square!
