2. Grilling "Aurelius the Radiant" at North Gate (06:45–07:15 AM)

What the Party Will Ask / Do Aurelius's Smooth, Rehearsed Reply Secret Tell / Monocle of Investigation
"Why did your charm blow up and kill us?!" Feigns solemn shock: "Ah—you felt the fiend's death throes! The charm worked as intended, purging a corrupted vessel. Stand clear when the tether snaps—its dark fire lashes out when broken!" Insight (Adv w/ Monocle of Investigation, DC 14): His concern feels rehearsed; faint amusement flickers in his golden eyes—he is eager for the beast to die, not worried about them.
"Forbidden by whom? Who are you?" "By the Concord of the Morning Spire. Celestial envoys may guide mortal champions, never spill infernal blood directly upon this soil." Religion / Insight (DC 14): "Concord of the Morning Spire" sounds archaic and subtly wrong—he dodges specific Lathanderite liturgy and smells faintly of cinnamon + sulfur.
"Why do you cast no shadow? Come into the Cathedral!" Chuckles smoothly: "Radiant envoys bend the solstice glare. Alas, I must keep watch at the road—do not waste precious minutes in temple halls while the beast wreaks havoc!" Hard Tell: Smoothly refuses to set foot on Cathedral steps. Remember New Canon: NO normal spell (*Detect Magic*, *Zone of Truth*, etc.) can unmask him—ONLY piercing his skin with a blessed weapon or under *Bless*!
"Where is this 'confused Angel'?" "Drifting west toward the sunlit sea cliffs, blinded by the devil's tether. Break the devil in the Ash Grove and the angel's mind clears!" Notice the Contradiction: Matches Jonas's *"Blame not the angel for he is confused"*—until they realize *which* creature is actually the Angel!

3. If They Shoot the Crucible Charm from >85 ft (Ash Grove — Hex 7, 6)

What Happens at the Grove (10:30–11:20 AM):
  • Dropping Archdruid Faelan's Name Early: Saying "Archdruid Faelan" before he introduces himself stuns him ("How do you know my name?!") and earns Advantage on Persuasion—he explains the druids aren't summoning the beast, they're trying to contain it because it fell from the sky at dawn!
  • Crucial Soft Clues Before the Shot: As they line up the arrow, the chained "devil" smells strongly of temple frankincense, roses & morning rain—and cries telepathically in pained Celestial: "Stop... the golden one lied... look to the sea...!"
  • The Arrow Hits from 100 ft: The party survives! The 85-ft blast still vaporizes the inner druid circle and leaves the exact same glassed obsidian crater.
The Big Payoff — Proving the Trap Failed!
  • Heat STILL Escalates (Even Faster!): By 1:00 PM it still hits 125°F sweltering, and by 3:00 PM it hits 175°F lethal! That proves 100% that killing the "devil" did NOT stop the heat—in fact, the True Angel's death broke the only thing slowing the solar siphon!
  • Flight Rule Reminder (Gwen's Polymorph Quetzalcoatl & Owl): Early-morning flight (06:00–10:00 AM) works great (1 hr Concentration, INT 2 for Quetzalcoatl). After 10:00 AM, violent upper-sky thermal updrafts make overland flight too dangerous/impossible.
  • If They Head Back to Beregost at Noon: You finally get to run the 1:00–5:30 PM Cathedral Crypt Evacuation & "Daisies" Tragedy (see Page 2)!
