# 🎧 Beregost 5:30 PM Crypt Audio Design & Mixing Guide
**Scene**: Session 1 (Loop 0) — 17:30 PM Crypt Tragedy & The Singing Devil  
**Tracks**: *"Daisies"* (Black Gryph0n & Baasik) + Crypt Ambience (Fire, Choking, Sobbing)

---

## 🎭 The Dramatic Concept: Intimate Horror vs. Muffled Taunt
Instead of blasting the song directly over the table (which can sound like an out-of-place pop track), place the **song muffled in the background** as if echoing down a 200-foot bell tower shaft through thick stone walls, while the **visceral sounds of the dying townsfolk (coughing, sobbing, roaring heat)** are right in the foreground.

This turns upbeat electro-swing into chilling psychological horror (the classic *BioShock* / *Fallout* effect).

---

## 📦 Required Audio Assets
1. **The Song**: *"Daisies"* by Black Gryph0n & Baasik (Trim to Verse 1 + Chorus: ~1 minute).
2. **Fire Ambience**: Search YouTube or [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org) for *"burning building low rumble"* or *"distant roaring fire crackle"*.
3. **Smoke & Choking SFX**: Search *"coughing in smoke sound effect"* or *"throat clearing gasping"*.
4. **Crowd Sobbing**: Search *"muffled crowd crying in bunker"* or *"quiet sobbing ambience"*.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Mixing Guide (Using Free [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/))

### 1. Import & Trim
* Open Audacity and drag in all 4 audio files.
* Trim the *"Daisies"* track so it starts at Verse 1 (*"Hey pal, hey friend..."*) and cuts out after the first chorus (*"...pushin' up daisies where the sun don't shine"*).

### 2. The "Muffled Through Stone" Filter (Crucial Step)
Select the **Daisies** track:
1. **Low-Pass Filter**:
   * Navigate to: `Effect -> EQ and Filters -> Low-Pass Filter`
   * **Cutoff Frequency**: **800 Hz** to **1,200 Hz** (Roll-off: 12 dB or 24 dB).
   * *Effect*: Strips away crisp cymbals and high treble, making it sound like it is trapped behind 2-foot stone vault walls.
2. **Cavernous Reverb**:
   * Navigate to: `Effect -> Delay and Reverb -> Reverb`
   * **Room Size**: **80%** (Cathedral/Large Hall)
   * **Reverberance / Decay**: **65%**
   * **Wet Gain**: **-6 dB**
   * *Effect*: Adds a distant, hollow echo, as if the sound is floating down the belfry air shaft.
3. **Volume Balance**:
   * Set the track volume slider on the left to **-8 dB to -12 dB** so it sits underneath the crying and coughing.

### 3. Layering the Crypt Atmosphere
1. **Distant Fire & Subterranean Rumble**: Set to **-4 dB**. This provides a constant, ominous bass presence in the room.
2. **Coughing & Choking SFX**: Space out coughs every 4–8 seconds across the timeline. Pan one cough 30% Left and another 30% Right for stereo immersion.
3. **Tobias & Maya Weeping**: Place soft whimpers in the quieter valleys of the music.

### 4. Dynamic Volume Automation (The Climax Reveal)
Press **F2** in Audacity to select the **Envelope Tool**:
* **Verse 1 (*"Hey pal, hey friend..."*)**: Keep the song quiet and muffled (around -10 dB). Players should hear the tragedy first, only faintly noticing someone singing upstairs.
* **Chorus (*"Stop and smell the roses..."*)**: Gradually raise the volume by **+4 dB** as the chorus hits.
* **In-Game Narrative Tie-In**: Describe the mortar in the stone cellar cracking under thermal expansion, dropping dust as the fiendish voice cuts through clearer:
  > *"Pretty soon you'll be pushin' up daisies where the sun don't shine..."*

### 5. Export
* Export the project as **`530pm_crypt_tragedy.mp3`**.

## 🎲 Running It at the Table
1. When the timeline hits **5:30 PM**, dim the lights (or switch room lights to deep orange/amber).
2. Describe the unbearable heat, blistering armor, and Tobias handing his last honey roll to crying 8-year-old Maya.
3. Press play on your mixed audio track at medium-low volume.
4. Let the audio play for 45–60 seconds without speaking. Watching your players realize that a cheerful, mocking Broadway villain song is echoing over their impending doom will give them goosebumps!
